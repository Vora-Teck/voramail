Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

At VoraMX, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our email delivery platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and company details when you sign up.
  • Usage Data: API calls, email volume, and feature usage.
  • Email Content: The content of emails you send through our platform (for delivery purposes only).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our email delivery service
  • Process your transactions and send emails on your behalf
  • Send important service updates and security notifications
  • Analyze usage to enhance user experience

3. Data Security & Encryption

Your Data is Protected

All email messages sent through VoraMX are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3. We also encrypt sensitive data at rest. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following cases:

  • With service providers who assist in delivering our service (e.g., email infrastructure partners)
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • In the event of a business merger or acquisition

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request data portability

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to operate the service and analytics cookies to understand how our platform is used. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. Email content is retained only as long as needed for delivery and analytics.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
